# Sangguniang Bayan — Municipality of Tanza

The **Sangguniang Bayan** (Municipal Council) is the legislative body of the Municipality of Tanza, Cavite. It enacts ordinances, approves resolutions, and appropriates funds to support the development and welfare of the community.

---

## About the Sangguniang Bayan

The Sangguniang Bayan is composed of **elected councilors** and **ex-officio members**, with the **Vice Mayor** serving as the presiding officer of all sessions. It works closely with the Office of the Mayor to pass legislation supporting Tanza's development plans.

**Vice Mayor and Presiding Officer:** Raymundo A. Del Rosario

## Functions and Powers

The Sangguniang Bayan:

- Enacts municipal ordinances and resolutions
- Approves the Annual Municipal Budget
- Authorizes the acquisition and sale of municipal property
- Levies taxes, fees, and charges as allowed by law
- Establishes development programs for the municipality
- Provides for the delivery of basic services and facilities

---

## Sessions

The Sangguniang Bayan holds **regular sessions** at the Municipal Hall in accordance with the Local Government Code. Special sessions may be called as needed. Residents are welcome to attend open sessions.
