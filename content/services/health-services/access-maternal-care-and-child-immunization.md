# Maternal Care & Child Immunization — Municipality of Tanza

The Municipal Health Office (MHO) and barangay health centers of Tanza provide **prenatal care**, **family planning**, and **free routine child immunization** for residents.

---

## Maternal Care (Prenatal & Related Services)

| Service                                                          | Where                   | Schedule                              | Requirements                            | Cost     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Prenatal check-ups (midwife/nurse; physician referral as needed) | Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM (varies by center) | Prenatal record (issued at first visit) | **Free** |
| Family Planning counseling & methods                             | Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–4 PM                    | Personal appearance for counseling      | **Free** |

**Tip:** Bring any previous prenatal card/records and a valid ID. If you're new, staff will register you and issue the needed record at the health center.

---

## Child Immunization (EPI)

Routine childhood vaccines are provided **free** under the Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI).

| Age/Visit                          | Vaccines                                           | Where                   | Regular Day/Time                        | Requirements                      | Cost     |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------- |
| Birth–12 months (per DOH schedule) | BCG, Pentavalent, OPV/IPV, MMR, Hepatitis B, PCV13 | Barangay Health Centers | Wednesdays, 8 AM–4 PM (except holidays) | ECCD card (issued on first visit) | **Free** |

> First time? The health worker will **issue an ECCD card** and guide you on next-dose return dates.

---

## How to Avail (Quick Steps)

1. **Go to your Barangay Health Center** during clinic hours (or the posted EPI day for vaccines).
2. **Register/Update your record:** Prenatal record for mothers, ECCD card for children (issued if first time).
3. **Receive service:** Prenatal assessment, counseling, or child vaccination per EPI schedule.
4. **Book your return date** for follow-ups and next vaccine doses as advised by the health staff.

---

## Notes

- Medical consultations at health centers are **free** (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM). Useful if you need a doctor's assessment alongside prenatal care.
- The MHO and health centers also run **mother's classes** and promote **facility-based delivery**; ask your midwife for schedules.
