# Free Health Services — Municipality of Tanza

The Municipal Health Office (MHO) of Tanza, Cavite offers free **check-ups, basic medicines, and vaccines** through the Rural Health Unit (RHU) and barangay health centers across the municipality.

---

## 1. Free Medical Check-ups

| Service              | Where                                             | Schedule                                     | Requirements                                              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| General consultation | Tanza Rural Health Unit / Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM (varies by health center) | Individual Treatment Record (ITR) — issued on first visit |

**Where to go:**

- **Tanza Rural Health Unit (RHU)**, Municipal Hall Compound, Tanza, Cavite 4108
- Your nearest **Barangay Health Center**

---

## 2. Free Basic Medicines

| Service                            | Where                            | Schedule           | Requirements                      |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Free basic medicines               | Tanza Rural Health Unit pharmacy | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM | Doctor's prescription or referral |
| Senior citizen medicine assistance | RHU / Barangay Health Centers    | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM | Senior Citizen ID + prescription  |

---

## 3. Free Vaccines & Immunization

| Beneficiary       | Vaccine/Service                                          | Where                   | Schedule                        | Requirements                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Children          | BCG, Pentavalent, OPV/IPV, MMR, Hepatitis B, PCV13 (EPI) | Barangay Health Centers | Typically Wednesdays, 8 AM–4 PM | ECCD card (issued onsite if first time) |
| Pregnant women    | Tetanus Toxoid vaccine + supplements                     | Barangay Health Centers | During prenatal services        | Proof of pregnancy, prenatal record     |
| Animal bite cases | Anti-rabies vaccine (first dose free)                    | Tanza Rural Health Unit | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM              | Proof of Tanza residency                |

---

## How to Avail

1. **Check-ups** → Go to your Barangay Health Center or the Tanza RHU; first-timers will be issued an ITR.
2. **Medicines** → Bring a doctor's prescription/referral; submit at RHU pharmacy window.
3. **Child immunization** → Visit your barangay health center on immunization day with ECCD card.
4. **Pregnancy services** → Attend prenatal visits at the barangay health center for free vaccine and supplements.
5. **Anti-rabies** → Report to the Tanza RHU; bring proof of residency.
