# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Municipality of Tanza

Below are **LGU-led** and **national programs** available to students and families in **Tanza, Cavite**: school supplies, student employment, scholarships, and educational assistance.

---

## 1) Free School Supplies (SEF-funded)

**What it is**

- The LGU provides **free school supplies** to public school learners using the **Special Education Fund (SEF)**, announced each school year with distribution schedules.

**How to access**

1. Watch your school's advisory and **DepEd Tanza I and II Districts** memos for distribution schedules.
2. Learners (with parent/guardian) claim supplies at the school on the announced date.

---

## 2) Student Employment — SPES (via Tanza PESO)

**What it is**

- The **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)** lets poor but deserving students and out-of-school youth (aged 15–30) work during semester breaks to earn income and gain experience.
- Implemented locally through the **Tanza Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** with DOLE.

**How to access**

1. Follow **LGU Tanza Cavite** on Facebook for SPES application windows and job fair advisories.
2. Prepare IDs, school documents, and income/indigency proof per announcement.
3. Submit at the PESO office at Tanza Municipal Hall; attend orientation when shortlisted.

---

## 3) LGU Scholarships & Educational Assistance

**What it is**

- The Tanza LGU **periodically opens scholarship calls** for local students and provides educational cash assistance for qualified residents.

**How to access**

1. Monitor **LGU Tanza Cavite** Facebook page and the **Tanza MSWDO** for official scholarship or assistance calls.
2. Prepare residency and academic documents; submit by deadline; attend screening/orientation.

---

## 4) DSWD Educational Assistance (AICS)

**What it is**

- **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)** for educational needs is nationally run by DSWD but coordinated locally through the **Tanza MSWDO**.

**How to access**

1. Visit the **Tanza MSWDO** at the Municipal Hall for walk-in assistance or referral.
2. Bring student ID, enrollment proof, and required documents.
