# Apply for Local Scholarships — Municipality of Tanza

Use this guide to find **LGU scholarship programs**, **congressional scholarships**, and **national programs** you can apply for as a resident of Tanza, Cavite.

---

## 1) LGU Scholarship Programs

| Program                                    | Who it's for                                                           | Where to apply                                    | Notes                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tanza Municipal Government Scholarship** | Tanza residents; priority to low-income households with academic merit | Tanza MSWDO / Office of the Mayor, Municipal Hall | Watch for official calls on the LGU Facebook page; slots and coverage vary per year [UNVERIFIED — please verify current scholarship program details with MSWDO] |

**How to apply (typical flow)**

1. Watch for the **official call for applications** from the Tanza LGU on [facebook.com/LGUTanzaCavite](https://www.facebook.com/LGUTanzaCavite).
2. Prepare documents (see Common Requirements below).
3. Submit your application at the **MSWDO window** and attend evaluation/orientation if shortlisted.

## Common Requirements (have these ready)

- **Proof of residency** (Barangay Certificate from your Tanza barangay)
- **Birth certificate** (PSA copy)
- **Report card/grades** (latest school year)
- **Good moral certificate** (from school)
- **Income documents** (parents' ITR or Certificate of Non-Filing or Indigency Certificate from MSWDO)
- **Photos** (2×2 ID photos)
- **Admission/Enrollment proof** (if already admitted to a school)

---

## Step-by-Step

1. **Pick your target programs** — LGU scholarship (watch Facebook), school-based scholarship, and at least one national program.
2. **Check the current call** — read the eligibility, coverage, and deadline; save the announcement.
3. **Assemble documents** — request certificates early from your barangay, school registrar, and MSWDO.
4. **Submit** — follow the specified portal or walk-in window; keep digital scans as backup.
5. **Monitor & attend screening** — answer calls/emails, attend orientations, and sign the scholarship agreement if accepted.
6. **Comply every term** — maintain the required GWA, submit grade reports on time, and renew each semester/year.
