# Apply for Local Scholarships — Tanza

## Municipal Scholarship (Municipality of Tanza)

The Municipality of Tanza accepts **scholarship applications through the [Tanza Public Service System](https://tanzapublicservice.site)** (service: _Processing of Scholarship Application_). The portal lists the service as **free**.

**How to apply**

1. **Register** a resident account on the portal (personal details, barangay and address, proof of residency) and wait for the municipality to approve it.
2. **Sign in** and open **Services** to find _Processing of Scholarship Application_. The specific requirements and schedule are shown there after sign-in.
3. **Track your request** under **My Requests**, and watch the portal's announcements for application calls.
4. For questions, use **Feedback & Inquiry** in the portal or visit the **Mayor's Office** at the Municipal Hall.

> The portal does not publicly list eligibility rules, slots, or deadlines. Always follow the current call posted by the municipality.

For other municipal assistance, see [Apply for Financial Assistance](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-financial-assistance).

## National Programs You Can Apply For From Tanza

| Program                           | Who it's for                                                             | Typical coverage                                     | Where to file                     |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **CHED Merit Scholarship (CMSP)** | Incoming or continuing college students (merit + income)                 | Stipend plus book/tuition assistance, per CHED rules | CHED's official calls and portals |
| **DOST-SEI S&T Scholarship**      | Grade 12 / senior high graduates entering science and technology courses | Tuition and allowances, per DOST-SEI rules           | DOST-SEI official announcements   |
| **OWWA EDSP / ODSP**              | Qualified dependents of active OWWA members                              | Per OWWA's current guidelines                        | OWWA scholarship portal           |

Coverage and deadlines change every cycle. Read each program's current announcement.

## Common Requirements (have these ready)

- **Proof of residency** (Barangay Certificate)
- **Birth certificate** (PSA)
- **Report card or grades** (JHS / SHS / college)
- **Good moral certificate** (from school)
- **Income documents** (parents' ITR, Certificate of Non-Filing, or indigency certificate, depending on program)
- **Photos / ID** (2×2 or passport size)
- **Admission or enrollment proof** (if already admitted)

Exact lists vary per program; always follow the posted checklist.

---

## Tips

1. Apply to more than one program to improve your chances.
2. Request certificates early (barangay, school registrar).
3. Keep digital scans (PDF, JPG, or PNG) ready for online filing.
4. If accepted, keep the required grade average and submit grade reports on time to renew.
