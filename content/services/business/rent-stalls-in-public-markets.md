# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Municipality of Tanza

Tanza has public markets managed by the **Municipal Government of Tanza**. Residents and vendors can apply for stall rentals at the Tanza Public Market.

---

## Who Can Apply

- Residents of Tanza, Cavite
- Registered business operators with valid Mayor's Business Permit
- Vendors selling fresh produce, dry goods, food, and other permitted merchandise

---

## How to Apply

1. **Inquire at the Tanza Public Market Administration Office** or at the **Municipal Hall** (General Services Office / Market Administration)
2. **Submit an application** with the following requirements:
   - Accomplished Application Form
   - Valid ID
   - Community Tax Certificate (CTC / Cedula)
   - Barangay Clearance
   - Mayor's Business Permit (if already operating)
3. **Await assessment** — stall assignment is based on availability and compliance with market regulations.
4. **Pay rental fees** — market stall rental rates are set by the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
5. **Comply with market rules** — follow segregation, sanitation, and operating hour requirements.

---

## Market Rules & Reminders

- Operating hours are set by the Market Administration; follow posted schedules.
- Maintain cleanliness and proper waste segregation in your stall area.
- Vendors must have updated health cards (from the Municipal Health Office).
- Subletting of stalls without LGU approval is prohibited.
