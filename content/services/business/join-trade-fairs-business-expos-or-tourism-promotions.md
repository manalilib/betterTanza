# Participate in Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotions — Municipality of Tanza

Showcase your products and services, meet buyers, and boost your brand by joining **municipal and regional trade fairs, business expos, and tourism promotions**.

---

## Who Can Join

- MSMEs (sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, cooperatives) based in Tanza
- Tourism enterprises (accommodations, tour guides, transport, event suppliers)
- Cultural creators (handicrafts, fashion, food, wellness)
- LGU-accredited community groups (livelihood associations, women/youth groups)

---

## Event Types

- **Municipal/Barangay Trade Fairs** — tied to the Tanza Town Fiesta (August 27–28), civic celebrations, and holidays
- **Business Expos & Startup Showcases** — buyers' meets, B2B networking, product launches
- **Tourism Promotion Activities** — travel marts, festivals, MICE activations
- **Sector-Specific Bazaars** — food & beverage, fashion & crafts, livelihood products

---

## How to Participate

1. **Prepare eligibility documents**
   - DTI/SEC/CDA registration, BIR Form 2303
   - Mayor's/Business Permit (current year)
   - Product compliance (FDA/LTO/CPR for food & cosmetics if applicable)

2. **Watch for official calls**
   - Follow **LGU Tanza Cavite** on Facebook for expo and trade fair announcements.
   - Coordinate with the **Tanza Municipal Trade and Industry / BPLO** for upcoming events.

3. **Submit Application/Intent Form**
   - Provide business profile, product list, booth needs, and preferred dates.
   - Once accepted, pay booth fees (if any) and sign guidelines.

4. **Pre-event readiness**
   - Bring banners, price cards, brochures, and QR codes.
   - Ensure packaging, labeling, and permits are compliant.

5. **Post-event follow-up (within 72 hours)**
   - Contact leads, confirm orders, and plan reorders.

---

## Program Benefits

- Subsidized booth fees for local MSMEs
- Product development mentoring (branding, packaging, costing)
- Media exposure through LGU press and social media
