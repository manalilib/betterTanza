# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Municipality of Tanza

Use this guide to renew your **Mayor's/Business Permit** and pay **Local Business Taxes (LBT)** with the Tanza Municipal Government.

---

## Who Must Renew

All businesses operating in Tanza: sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, NGOs, cooperatives, and professionals with a business office. Renewal applies to the main office and each branch.

---

## When to Renew

- **Annual renewal period:** First 20 days of January (as provided in the Local Government Code).
- **Quarterly payments:** 1st quarter due during renewal; subsequent quarters due on or before the 20th of April, July, and October.
- Late filings incur **surcharges and interest**.

> Tip: Prepare your documents by December so you can file in the first week of January.

---

## What You'll Need

**Business Information**

- Prior year's Mayor's/Business Permit and Official Receipts
- DTI/SEC/CDA registration
- BIR Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)
- Contract of Lease / Tax Declaration / Certificate of Occupancy (as applicable)
- Valid IDs of owner or authorized representative (+ SPA or Board Resolution if not the owner)

**Revenue/Tax Basis**

- Sworn Declaration of Gross Sales/Receipts for the preceding year
- Audited Financial Statements (if applicable)

**Regulatory Clearances**

- Barangay Clearance (for business)
- Sanitary Permit (Municipal Health Office)
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate — FSIC (BFP)
- Zoning Clearance (Municipal Planning and Development Office — MPDO)

---

## Step-by-Step (Walk-In at Tanza Municipal Hall)

1. **Go to the BPLO** at Tanza Municipal Hall Compound during the renewal period.
2. **Fill out the Business Renewal Form** and Sworn Declaration of Gross Sales/Receipts.
3. **Declare any changes** (address, business nature, floor area, capitalization, employee count).
4. **Regulatory routing** — the evaluator routes you to Health, Zoning, BFP, and Barangay as needed.
5. **Assessment** — BPLO computes Local Business Tax, Regulatory Fees, and Other Charges.
6. **Payment** — pay at the Municipal Treasurer's Office; receive Official Receipts.
7. **Claiming** — claim your Mayor's/Business Permit, FSIC, Sanitary Permit, and stickers; post conspicuously at place of business.

---

## How Your Dues Are Computed

- **Local Business Tax (LBT):** Based on gross sales/receipts of the prior year and your business category.
- **Regulatory Fees:** Sanitary, Zoning, Garbage, Signage, Occupational, and Fire fees.

---

## Penalties for Late Renewal

- Surcharge (up to **25%** of tax due) + monthly interest (up to **2% per month**) per LGU ordinance.
- Possible closure order if you continue operations without a valid permit.
