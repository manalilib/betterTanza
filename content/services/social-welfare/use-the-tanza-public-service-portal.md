# Online Municipal Services — Tanza Public Service System

The Municipality of Tanza runs an online resident portal, the **[Tanza Public Service System](https://tanzapublicservice.site)**. Residents can submit service requests, track transactions, and receive municipal updates online.

---

## Services Available Through the Portal

All of the following are listed as **free** on the portal.

| Service                                                                                                         | What it covers                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Solemnization of Marriage**                                                                                   | Solemnization of marriage through the Municipality of Tanza                                                                                                    |
| **Reservation of Municipal Sports Complex**                                                                     | Reserve the Municipal Sports Complex                                                                                                                           |
| **Request for Permit to Bury at Public Cemetery**                                                               | Request a permit to bury at a public cemetery                                                                                                                  |
| **Provision of Financial Assistance** (Mayor's Office / Administrative Division)                                | Medical, burial, and sports/socio-cultural/literary assistance — see [Apply for Financial Assistance](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-financial-assistance) |
| **Processing of Scholarship Application**                                                                       | Processing of scholarship applications through the Municipality                                                                                                |
| **Issuance of Affidavits**                                                                                      | Frontline affidavit-related service of the Office of the Mayor                                                                                                 |
| **Issuance of Mayor's Clearance, Certification, Mayor's Permit / Occupational Permit, Referrals / Endorsement** | Frontline Office of the Mayor services for clearances, certifications, permits, referrals, and endorsements                                                    |

The portal publishes a processing time only for Financial Assistance (19 minutes). Requirements for the other services are shown after you sign in.

---

## Getting Access

1. **Register** — create your resident account with your personal details, barangay and address, and proof of residency.
2. **Wait for approval** — a municipal administrator reviews your registration. New accounts cannot use the portal until approved.
3. **Access services** — log in after approval.
4. **Track transactions** — monitor requests, notifications, and updates.

---

## Portal Features

- Online service requests
- Financial assistance applications
- Request status tracking
- Announcements and notifications
- Feedback and inquiries

---

## Good to Know

- Services and announcements are visible only after you sign in; the public pages are the home page and Help & FAQ.
- For questions about a request, use **Feedback & Inquiry** in the portal, or visit the **Mayor's Office** at the Municipal Hall.
