# Apply for Financial Assistance — Municipality of Tanza

The Municipality of Tanza provides **Provision of Financial Assistance** under its Citizen's Charter, handled by the **Mayor's Office / Administrative Division**. You can apply online through the [Tanza Public Service System](https://tanzapublicservice.site), upload your requirements, and follow your application until the assistance is released.

| Item               | Details                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can apply**  | Residents of Tanza, Cavite with an approved resident account                 |
| **Fee**            | Free                                                                         |
| **Listed time**    | 19 minutes (the portal's listed processing time for the service)             |
| **Where to apply** | https://tanzapublicservice.site, or the Mayor's Office at the Municipal Hall |

---

## Types of Assistance

- Medical assistance
- Burial assistance
- Sports tournament, socio-cultural, or literary competition assistance

The application form shows the exact requirements for the type you choose.

---

## Requirements

### Medical assistance

| Requirement                                   | Copies                  | Where to get it                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Letter of request                             | 1 original              | Prepared by you                 |
| Medical certificate or abstract               | 1 original or photocopy | Attending physician             |
| Billing, doctor's prescription, or lab result | 1 photocopy             | Hospital or attending physician |
| Barangay clearance                            | 1 original              | Your barangay                   |
| Barangay certificate of indigency             | 1 original              | Your barangay                   |

### Burial assistance

| Requirement                       | Copies                | Where to get it                             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Letter of request                 | 1 original            | Prepared by you                             |
| Death certificate                 | 1 certified true copy | Local Civil Registrar where the person died |
| Voter's certification             | 1 certified true copy | COMELEC Tanza                               |
| Barangay clearance                | 1 original            | Your barangay                               |
| Barangay certificate of indigency | 1 original            | Your barangay                               |

If the deceased was a **senior citizen**, also prepare their barangay residency (1 original) and a photocopy of their senior citizen ID.

### Sports, socio-cultural, or literary assistance

| Requirement                                           | Copies     | Where to get it                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Letter of request                                     | 1 original | Prepared by the requesting individual or group |
| Barangay clearance endorsement, or school endorsement | 1 original | Your barangay or school                        |

**Uploads:** PDF, JPG, or PNG files up to **5 MB each**. Make sure every page is complete, clear, and readable.

## How to Apply Online

1. **Register** on the portal: enter your personal details, barangay and address, and upload a **proof of residency**, then confirm your email with a one-time code.
2. **Wait for approval.** The municipality reviews every new account, and you are notified once it is approved.
3. **Sign in**, open **Services**, choose **Provision of Financial Assistance**, and start your request.
4. Complete the five short steps: your details, type of assistance, the beneficiary, your documents, and a final review.
5. Press **Submit**. Nothing is sent until you do. Your answers are kept only while you stay signed in, so if you sign out before submitting you will need to start again.

You may apply on behalf of a family member: name the beneficiary and your relationship to them. For burial assistance, enter the details of the deceased.

---

## What Happens After You Submit

Open **My Requests** to see which of the six steps your application is on:

1. Submitted
2. Documents checked
3. Assessment
4. Voucher and signing
5. Ready for release
6. Released

| Stage               | Who handles it                                                                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Document check      | Mayor's Office                                                                   |
| Assessment          | A social worker from the MSWDO (Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office) |
| Voucher             | Processed by the municipal office                                                |
| Signing and release | Mayor's Office signs the voucher and schedules the release                       |

You are notified at each main step. If a document is not accepted, your application page shows which one to replace and the reason. Upload a replacement for that document only. If the application is not approved, the page will say so.

---

## Release of Assistance

- The release date and time appear on your application page and you are notified.
- Go to the **Municipal Hall** on that schedule and bring a **valid ID**.
- If you are claiming for a beneficiary, bring any additional documents named in your notification.
- The **amount** is decided during the assessment under the municipality's guidelines. It is not set when you apply.

---

## Need Help?

- Use **Feedback & Inquiry** in the resident portal and mention your request reference number.
- Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password** on the login page; a one-time code is sent to your registered email.
- You may also visit the **Mayor's Office** at the Municipal Hall.
